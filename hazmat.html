How to Use the ERG — Step by Step

Step 1

Identify the material — use placard, label, shipping papers, or orange panel UN number

Step 2

Look up UN number in YELLOW pages or material name in BLUE pages to find guide number

Step 3

If material is highlighted GREEN, also check GREEN pages for isolation distances

Step 4

Turn to ORANGE pages — find the guide number. Read Potential Hazards, Public Safety, Emergency Response

Skin Protective Clothing Categories

Thermal Protective

Structural firefighter clothing, proximity suits, fire-entry suits, flame-resistant clothing, low-temperature/cryogenic clothing

Chemical Protective (CPC)

Vapor-protective (encapsulating), liquid splash-protective (encapsulating or nonencapsulating). No single CPC protects against all chemicals.

Ballistic Protective

Body armor, bomb suits. Protects against bullets, shrapnel, handheld weapons. Does NOT protect against chemical hazards.

CPC Failure Modes

Permeation

Chemical passes through fabric at molecular level. No visible evidence. Decon will NOT stop permeation.

Degradation

Physical or chemical breakdown of suit material. Signs: discoloration, swelling, loss of strength.

Penetration

Hazmat enters through a physical opening — rips, tears, cuts, unsealed seams, zippers.
