Weather Quick Facts

Standard atmosphere15 C and 29.92 inHg at sea level; cools about 2 C per 1,000 ft
Stable airStratiform clouds, steady rain, smooth air, fair to poor visibility
Unstable airCumuliform clouds, showers, turbulence, good visibility
Thunderstorm needsMoisture, unstable air, and lift. Stages: cumulus, mature (rain starts), dissipating (downdrafts)
MicroburstDowndrafts up to 6,000 fpm, usually lasting 15 minutes or less
FogRadiation (clear calm nights), advection (moist air over cooler ground), upslope, steam
Density altitudeHigh, hot, and humid = less thrust and shorter flights
FrontsCold: fast, showers and storms. Warm: slow, layered clouds and steady rain

Getting Your Certificate

1. Get an FTNCreate an FAA Tracking Number in IACRA
2. ScheduleBook the UAG test at an FAA-approved testing center (must be 16)
3. Pass60 questions, 2 hours, 70 percent
4. ApplyComplete FAA Form 8710-13 in IACRA with your test ID
5. TSA checkPrint a temporary certificate (valid 120 days) when it clears
6. Stay currentFree online recurrent training every 24 calendar months
