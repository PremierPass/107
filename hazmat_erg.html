ERG Color-Coded Sections
YELLOW PAGES

Index by ID Number

Look up materials by 4-digit UN/NA identification number in numerical order. Points you to the correct guide number.

BLUE PAGES

Index by Name

Look up materials alphabetically by proper shipping name. Points you to the correct guide number.

ORANGE PAGES

Action Guides

The actual response guides. Contains: Potential Hazards, Public Safety, Emergency Response. Most useful section.

GREEN PAGES

Isolation Distances

Table of Initial Isolation and Protective Action Distances for TIH materials and water-reactive materials. Use for green-highlighted materials.

WHITE PAGES (FRONT)

User Guide & Reference

ERG User's Guide flowchart, how to use the ERG, placard/label reference tables, container profiles, pipeline markers.

WHITE PAGES (BACK)

WMD & Emergency Contacts

WMD indicators, IED information, emergency response center contact numbers (CHEMTREC, CANUTEC, SETIQ).

How to Use the ERG — Step by Step

Step 1

Identify the material — use placard, label, shipping papers, or orange panel UN number

Step 2

Look up UN number in YELLOW pages or material name in BLUE pages to find guide number

Step 3

If material is highlighted GREEN, also check GREEN pages for isolation distances

Step 4

Turn to ORANGE pages — find the guide number. Read Potential Hazards, Public Safety, Emergency Response
